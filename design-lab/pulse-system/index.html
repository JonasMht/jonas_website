Pulse·Fusion 2 — design system

Every element, and why it exists.

Fusion 2 is not a pile of components — it is one system: a control-room reading of a researcher's life. This dashboard documents every element, what it does, and the rule that keeps the whole coherent.

00

The system

Every page answers one question per layer. A visitor who reads nothing still gets layer 1; a visitor who taps everything reaches layer 4. Elements never mix layers.

L1

Identity — "who is this?"

Photo with focus brackets, name, role, one bold statement. Above the fold, always.

every landing
L2

Orientation — "what are the numbers?"

Telemetry cells: publications, speed, output. Mono voice, right-aligned values.

hero · hubs
L3

Browse — "what's interesting?"

Image-led module cards. One line of meaning each; scan in seconds.

hubs · home
L4

Depth — "tell me more."

Expandable dossiers, logs and sessions. Full detail, only on demand.

07

Focus brackets

The EdelVideo corner-tick frame — full border plus doubled L-ticks at all four corners — is the system's only "shout". Budget: three per page, maximum.

NOT ALLOWED · DECOR

SYSTEM / bracket budget

Brackets mark meaning, not boxes

Allowed: the identity portrait, the featured module image, the single key figure. Never: chrome, cards, telemetry, footers — those speak through hairlines.

≤ 3 bracketed elements per page.when in doubt, unbracket
