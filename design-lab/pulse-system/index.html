TOKENS / voice

Sans speaks. Mono reports.

Sans is the human voice: headlines, reading text. Mono caps is the machine voice: telemetry, labels, chips. The two never cross — mono never writes prose, sans never shouts labels.

One accent word per headline, max.labels are letterspaced 0.12–0.16em, caps, faint

07

Focus brackets

The EdelVideo corner-tick frame — full border plus doubled L-ticks at all four corners — is the system's only "shout". The ticks float ~8px off the element: a deliberate moat of dark ground between bracket and image, so even the brightest picture cannot hide them. Budget: three per page, maximum.

ALLOWED · IDENTITY
NOT ALLOWED · DECOR

SYSTEM / bracket budget

Brackets mark meaning, not boxes

Allowed: the identity portrait, the featured module image, the single key figure. Never: chrome, cards, telemetry, footers — those speak through hairlines.

≤ 3 bracketed elements per page.when in doubt, unbracket

Implementation notes

Ticks are one ::before with eight background gradients — no extra DOM, no images. The moat is inset:-8px; size it with the element, not the viewport. Radius on the image, never on the bracket box.
