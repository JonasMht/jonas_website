§ 01

Problem statement

Percutaneous thermal ablation treats small liver tumors well — until one needle isn't enough. Clinicians then plan overlapping ablations on 2D CT slices, and local recurrence reaches 39.1 % at five years. My PhD makes multi-needle planning computable, adaptive, and clinically usable.

§ 02

Method — three axes

A1

Multi-needle trajectory optimization

Exhaustive and learning-based placement under anatomical constraints; search-space reduction, adaptive simulation resolution, DL-based ablation volume estimation.

A2

Intraoperative replanning

Tracking of inserted needles, dynamic estimation of the partly treated volume, plan adaptation for subsequent insertions.

A3

Ergonomic planning interface

A GUI for planning, tracking and replanning — validated through usability studies with clinical experts and novices.
