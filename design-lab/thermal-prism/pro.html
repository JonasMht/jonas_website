Research axes

Axis 01

Multi-needle trajectory optimization

Exhaustive and learning-based placement under anatomical constraints; search-space reduction and DL-based ablation volume estimation.

Axis 02

Intraoperative replanning

Track inserted needles, estimate the partly treated volume, adapt the plan while the procedure unfolds.

Axis 03

Ergonomic planning interface

A GUI for planning, tracking and replanning — validated with clinicians and novices through usability studies.
