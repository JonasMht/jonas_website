Multi-needle trajectory optimization

Exhaustive vs. learning-based placement under anatomical constraints

ACTIVE▸

Comparing exhaustive search and learning-based methods for multi-needle placement, respecting vital structures. Search-space reduction, adaptive simulation resolution, and deep-learning ablation-volume estimation feed the planning loop.

axis 02 · replanning

Intraoperative replanning

Track needles, estimate treated volume, adapt mid-procedure

ACTIVE▸

The OR doesn't wait for offline planning. Tracking inserted needles and estimating the partly treated volume lets the plan adapt for the next insertion — while the procedure unfolds.
