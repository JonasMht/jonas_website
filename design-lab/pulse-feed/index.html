Planning heat before it burns

My PhD in one paragraph — the problem, the approach, the numbers.

PINNED▸

Percutaneous thermal ablation treats small liver tumors — until one needle isn't enough. Manual multi-needle planning on 2D CT lets recurrence climb to 39.1%. I build the planning loop that fixes this: GPU simulation under a second (HEAT, IJCARS 2025), neural estimation at 476 fps (C-NCA, MICCAI 2025), and an interface clinicians validated with their own hands.
