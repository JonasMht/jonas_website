The workshop never closes.

Games that started as exam projects and refused to stop. Worlds in miniature for a museum. Tools against tiny annoyances. Everything here was built after dark.

The workshop's founding principle: when something annoying needs solving, build a tool for it — and log it in side quests.txt. Its second principle: build worlds anyway. The collection below spans six years of nights.
