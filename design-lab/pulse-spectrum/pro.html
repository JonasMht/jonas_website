The hot end: thermal ablation, plannable in real time.

When a liver tumor outgrows one needle, clinicians plan overlapping ablations by hand — and recurrence reaches 39.1% at five years. My PhD at ICube (ITI HealthTech program, supervised by C. Essert & J. Verde) builds the planning loop that fixes this.

Three research axes

all active
A1

Multi-needle trajectory optimization

▸
Exhaustive and learning-based placement under anatomical constraints — search-space reduction, adaptive simulation resolution, and DL-based ablation volume estimation feeding an interactive optimization loop.
A2

Intraoperative replanning

▸
Live adaptation: track inserted needles, estimate the partly treated volume, and re-plan for the next insertion while the procedure unfolds.
A3

Ergonomic planning interface

▸
Validated with humans: a planning/tracking/replanning GUI studied with clinical experts and novices — because a planner nobody enjoys is a planner nobody runs.
