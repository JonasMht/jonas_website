The three axes

My PhD proceeds on three fronts. First, multi-needle trajectory optimization — comparing exhaustive and learning-based placement methods while respecting vital structures. Second, intraoperative replanning: tracking inserted needles and adapting the plan as the procedure unfolds. Third, an ergonomic interface validated with the clinicians who actually use it.
