01 / Professional — Research Dossier

Thermal ablation,
plannable in
real time.

Percutaneous thermal ablation destroys small liver tumors with needles and heat. When one needle isn't enough, planning several overlapping ablations by hand invites recurrence rates of 10–39%. My PhD removes the guesswork.

A. Research axes

A1

Multi-needle trajectory optimization

Exhaustive vs. learning-based placement under anatomical constraints. Search-space reduction, adaptive simulation resolution, DL-based ablation volume estimation.

A2

Intraoperative replanning

Track inserted needles, estimate the partially treated volume, adapt the plan for the next insertion — while the procedure unfolds.

A3

Ergonomic planning interface

A GUI for planning, tracking and replanning, validated through usability studies with clinical experts and novices.
