Research axesthe three fronts

A1 — Multi-needle trajectory optimization

Exhaustive and learning-based placement under anatomical constraints. Search-space reduction, adaptive simulation resolution, DL-based ablation volume estimation — comparing methods head-to-head on clinical data.

A2 — Intraoperative replanning

Track inserted needles, estimate the partly treated volume dynamically, adapt the plan for the next insertion — while the patient is still on the table.

A3 — Ergonomic planning interface

A GUI for planning, tracking and replanning, validated through usability studies with clinical experts and novices alike.
