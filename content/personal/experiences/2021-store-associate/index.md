## Description
In 2021 I worked part-time for a year at the convenience store in my hometown.
<br>
I was working part-time on weekends and during the holidays at the local convenient store of my hometown.

## Mission
I was responsible for sales, stock management, supply trips, accounting, and order
preparation.
<br>
I had to perform all these tasks simultaneously and efficiently as I was the only employee in the store during my shifts.

## Conclusion
Solo shifts in a small store: sales, stock, accounting and order preparation, all on the same day.
