I'm a PhD researcher in computer-assisted interventions at ICube Laboratory, University of Strasbourg. I started my PhD in September 2024, supervised by Caroline Essert and Juan Verde, with funding through ITI HealthTech.

## Research

My PhD focuses on planning percutaneous thermal ablation with multiple needles. I study how simulation and optimisation can help compare placements, account for anatomical constraints and adapt a plan as needles are inserted.

The work brings together three areas:

- **Ablation prediction:** numerical simulation and learned models of thermal tissue damage.
- **Needle planning:** placement optimisation and adaptation during an intervention.
- **Interactive software:** interfaces for exploring medical images, treatment plans and tracked instruments.

My research publications include [C-NCA](/pro/research/cnca-2025/), [HEAT](/pro/research/2025-heat-ijcars/) and [Cryotrack](/pro/research/2024-cryotrack-miccai/). Each project page links to the paper, its evaluation and a citation.

## Background

Before the PhD, I worked on computer-assisted surgery during research internships at ICube, IHU Strasbourg and TU Darmstadt. Earlier projects included a usability study at the University of Oviedo and a landslide-data analysis tool at EOST in Strasbourg.
