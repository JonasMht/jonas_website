## Disclaimer
This internship is yet to be completed and the content of this post is subject to change.

## Context
The focus of my internship was centered on advancing computer-assisted surgery techniques in percutaneous cancer treatments, specifically exploring thermal ablation.
<br>
I developed a networked and accelerated heat simulation as well as an augmented reality (AR) integration for surgery preop and intraop visualization.
<br>
It lasted from the 29th of January to the 29th of August 2024 and was conducted in the IMAGeS research group of the ICube laboratory at the University of Strasbourg, France.

## Objectives
The primary goal of the internship was to develop an efficient heat simulation utilizing computer acceleration being capable of a networked deployment.
<br>
This networked system would allow various services, including augmented reality headsets and 3D visualization software to connect seamlessly, enhancing surgical visualization and interaction.

{{< figure src="networking.png" width=100% title="Network Layout">}}

Throughout the internship, I worked extensively with Python for prototyping, C++ for implementation and the 3D Slicer interface, learning to setup a networked solution utilizing a VPN for secure and private communication between the different equipment pieces. I tackled challenges related to GPU acceleration, code optimization and networked deployments.
<br><br>
The internship involved a deep dive into practical software development for minimally invasive surgery and treatment of cancer, contributing to the field of medical technology and patient care.

## Contributions
- Efficient heat simulation (data to be compiled)
- Networked deployment of the heat simulation
- Augmented reality integration
- 3D visualization software integration
