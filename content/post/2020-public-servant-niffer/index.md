## Description
Back in 2020, I was enrolled as a public servant in my hometown of Niffer for two consecutive years.
<br>
I was in charge of the surveillance of the recycling center, the maintenance of green spaces and the hauling and escorting of equipment.
<br>

## Missions

### Recycling Center : Surveillance and Maintenance
This mission consisted of receiving the users, keeping an eye out for proper sorting of the waste and maintaining the recycling center.

{{< figure src="recycling-center.jpg" title="Recycling center" >}}


### Maintenance of Green Spaces
On a daily basis, I was in charge of mowing the lawns, weeding the flower beds and watering the plants in various locations of the municipality.

{{< figure src="mower.jpg"   title="Mowing the football field" >}}

### Hauling and Escorting Equipment
I would drive drive various vehicles such as a tractor and a van for the purpose hauling equipment on site and escorting large heavy-duty vehicles.

{{< figure src="escort.jpg"   title="Escorting a tractor" >}}

## Conclusion
This experience allowed me to discover work as a public servant and to develop my skills in the field of teamwork, planning and versatile equipment use.
